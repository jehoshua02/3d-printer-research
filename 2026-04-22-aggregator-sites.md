# 3D Printer Aggregator & Comparison Sites

## Databases & Comparison Tools

- **[ProductChart](https://www.productchart.com/3d_printers/)** — Daily-updated interactive comparison chart. Filterable by spec and price. Best pure database tool.
- **[3DWork Comparator](https://3dwork.io/en/3d-product-comparator-detailed-analysis-and-recommendations/)** — FDM/resin comparator with specs, review scores, price.
- **[Aurora Tech Channel](https://auroratechchannel.com/)** — Hourly price tracking + specs comparison.

## Review Sites

- **[All3DP](https://all3dp.com/1/best-3d-printer-reviews-top-3d-printers-home-3-d-printer-3d/)** — Large review library, buyer's guides by category.
- **[Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/best-picks/best-3d-printers)** — Rigorous hands-on testing of 100+ printers.

## Community

- **[r/3Dprinting](https://reddit.com/r/3Dprinting)** — Largest 3D printing community on Reddit.
- **[Voron Design Forum](https://forum.vorondesign.com/)** — DIY/enthusiast toolchanger and printer discussion.
- **[Prusa Forum](https://forum.prusa3d.com/)** — Prusa-specific but covers toolchanging extensively.
